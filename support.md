# In Tune: Cycle Journal — Beta Support

In Tune is a private period and cycle journal for iPhone. This small TestFlight beta is free and requires iOS 27.2 or later. It includes built-in Care ideas. Apple Health isn't connected in this beta. Estimates are not medical advice or birth control.

## Report a problem

In TestFlight, open **In Tune: Cycle Journal** and choose **Send Beta Feedback**. Include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Synthetic examples are preferred. Redact screenshots; never send backup passphrases, device passcodes or encrypted backup files with your report.

Public support email: [intune@valioguard.com](mailto:intune@valioguard.com)

## Keep a recoverable copy

Before updating, open **More → Encrypted backup** in the app and save an encrypted backup to Files. Keep the passphrase privately and separately from the file. The developer cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

To restore, use **More → Encrypted backup → Restore from a backup**. Opening a backup by tapping it in Files may show a system preview; use the app's picker. **Replace** can replace existing history; review the preview and confirmation before applying it. **Add** keeps existing days and adds missing days. CSV/PDF exports are not restorable backups.

Do not uninstall the app to fix a problem: local history is excluded from iCloud/computer device backups, so uninstalling can lose it. Update in place. Saved backup/export copies remain wherever you saved or shared them and must be deleted separately.

## Known beta limits

Non-empty encrypted backup/restore has automated coverage with synthetic data; real two-phone/iCloud transfer remains unverified. On-device backup exclusion and file-protection checks remain unperformed. One initial tab hold/lift activation was reported to fail before later retries passed; no root cause was established. Further physical VoiceOver/large-text checks were deferred by the owner with simulator coverage accepted. Apple Health isn't part of this beta.

TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. Keep an encrypted backup and update when a new beta is provided.
