# In Tune: Cycle Journal — Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026.

intune stores period dates, day logs, symptoms, moods, energy, notes, tags, cycle settings and retained saved Care state on the iPhone. Care ideas are built into the app and work without a connection; intune doesn't send what you read or save. The app has no user account, server, advertising, tracking or analytics code and does not transmit logs to the developer.

This beta doesn't connect to Apple Health. It doesn't ask for Health access and doesn't read or write any Health data. This policy describes what intune does; Apple's own Health and iCloud settings are separate.

Logs and sensitive preferences are protected using iOS file protection and excluded from device backups. App lock uses iOS authentication; the app does not receive biometric templates or the device passcode. Optional local reminders are scheduled through iOS. Discreet wording is the default; explicit wording may reveal information to people who see notifications.

People may choose to save passphrase-encrypted backup files or share CSV/PDF exports. Exports are not passphrase-encrypted and may contain health information. A chosen file provider, cloud service or recipient controls copies outside the app. intune cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase. Keep encrypted backups before updates or uninstalling; device backups are not a recovery method for logged history.

Delete all data removes app logs, cycle settings, saved Care state and reminder choices while retaining app-lock settings. Saved/shared files must be deleted separately. Updating the app cannot remove older copies already held in historical device backups. Uninstalling removes local app history.

Apple provides TestFlight distribution, diagnostics and beta feedback under Apple's policies. Reports and screenshots a tester chooses to send may reach the developer. Please omit personal health details, backup files and passphrases. No health data is required to report a bug; synthetic examples are preferred.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments through the email provider to respond to your request. Do not send health records, backup files or passphrases.

These documentation pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website connection information under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This is separate from the app’s on-device storage.
